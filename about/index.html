About Jim Tooher

Jim Tooher is a Content Creator based in the Boston area.

He studied at the University of Massachusetts Amherst and has spent his career in Boston-area hospitality.

Outside of hospitality, James P. Tooher is the creator and host of the Jim Tooher Show, a comedy series he wrote and directed, and appeared as an actor in the 2013 short film Lucky John.
